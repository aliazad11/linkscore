Cookie Policy

Last updated 12 June 2026

LinkedScore uses a small amount of browser storage. We only set non-essential storage (analytics) after you opt in. You can change your choice at any time via Cookie settings in the footer.

Strictly necessary

These are always on, because the product cannot work without them. They are set by us (first-party) and store no advertising identifiers.

NamePurposeRetention
ls_funnel_v1Remembers your progress through the questionnaire so you can resume.Local storage, up to 7 days
ls_localeRemembers the language you chose. Functional / strictly necessary when you change it yourself.Local storage, until cleared
ls_consent_v1Remembers your cookie choice so we do not ask again.Local storage, until cleared

Analytics (optional, only with consent)

If you accept analytics, we load PostHog to understand how the product is used and improve it. PostHog runs on EU infrastructure. We do not load it at all unless you opt in, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

ProviderPurposeRegion
PostHogProduct and usage analytics (page views, feature use).EU Cloud

What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or sell your data. Booking a call opens Calendly only when you click the link; nothing from Calendly loads before that.

Managing your choice

Open Cookie settings in the footer to accept, reject, or change analytics at any time. You can also clear your browser storage. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture and your rights.
