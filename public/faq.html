Your scores and your plan

What scores do I get?
You get two, both out of 100. A LinkedIn Score rates your profile, how you show up before you ever post. A Thought Leader Score rates your content, whether your posts actually build authority.
How is the score calculated?
Your LinkedIn Score weighs the quality of your profile together with your SSI, LinkedIn's own Social Selling Index. Your Thought Leader Score reads your recent posts, their hooks, structure, voice and engagement. The more you give us, the sharper both get.
What is SSI, and do I need to know my numbers?
SSI is LinkedIn's Social Selling Index. It has four pillars, building your brand, finding the right people, engaging with insights and building relationships, each scored 0 to 25. If you know yours you can enter them for a sharper result. If not, you can skip them.
Do I have to upload my LinkedIn profile?
No, but it makes your plan far more accurate, because we critique what you actually wrote instead of guessing. Export it from LinkedIn (Resources, then Save to PDF) in about thirty seconds. Add a few recent post screenshots to unlock your Thought Leader Score.
Will it teach me how the LinkedIn algorithm works?
Yes, that is the core of it. Your plan explains in plain language how the algorithm rewards, buries and amplifies content, and the specific rules to play it on purpose.
What exactly is in the plan?
A rewritten headline, About and experience you can paste straight in, three post hooks in your voice, a 30-day content calendar, your SSI broken down with fixes, ready-to-send networking messages, and the handful of rules that actually move the needle.
